# Starter Output Examples — Agenda & Protokol (Ikhsan)

## Example 1 — Agenda Coordination Plan
- **Task shape:** prepare a pimpinan meeting or coordination event.
- **Expected artifact:** agenda plan with date, venue, participant grouping, protocol dependencies, and approval touchpoint.
- **Good output:** separates draft coordination from final confirmed schedule.

## Example 2 — Protocol Readiness Checklist
- **Task shape:** validate event readiness, VIP flow, or invitation sequencing.
- **Expected artifact:** protocol checklist covering room readiness, invite status, speaking order, and risk flags.
- **Good output:** names any blocker clearly and identifies who must confirm resolution.

## Example 3 — Escalation Note
- **Task shape:** high-profile event has unresolved owner, schedule conflict, or protocol sensitivity.
- **Expected artifact:** escalation-ready note with impact, missing decision, and accountable office.
