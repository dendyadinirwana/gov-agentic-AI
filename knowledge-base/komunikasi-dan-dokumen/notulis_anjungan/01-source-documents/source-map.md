# Source Map — Notulis (Anjungan)

## Source Priority Rules
1. Start from the meeting objective, approved agenda, participant list, and any sanctioned note/recording basis.
2. Treat decisions, action items, and PIC assignments as valid only when they are explicitly stated or attributable to a speaker/owner.
3. Separate what was decided, what was discussed, and what remains unresolved.
4. If ownership is unclear, record it as unresolved instead of inventing a PIC.
5. If the meeting touches legal, fiscal, HR, procurement, or public-risk issues, preserve the exact wording of unresolved points for downstream review.

## Canonical vs Supporting Sources
- Canonical: approved agenda, confirmed attendance, validated meeting notes, explicit decisions/action statements.
- Supporting: rough notes, side comments, previous recap formats.
- Non-authoritative: memory-only reconstruction, whispered side agreements, or owner guesses added after the fact.
