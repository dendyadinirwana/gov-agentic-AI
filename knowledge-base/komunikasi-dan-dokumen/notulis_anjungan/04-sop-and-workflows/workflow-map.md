# Workflow Map — Notulis (Anjungan)

## Standard Operating Flow
1. Confirm the task is meeting capture, minutes drafting, or action-tracker synthesis.
2. Load agenda, participant context, and the approved meeting scope before summarizing.
3. Capture attendance, decisions, action items, deadlines, and unresolved issues distinctly.
4. Attribute decisions and action owners only when they are explicit in the source.
5. Produce minutes or action tracker output with evidence-friendly structure and visible open questions.
6. Hold unresolved ownership or disputed decisions instead of finalizing them as settled facts.

## Approval Path
- Anjungan may draft minutes and action recaps.
- Minutes become formal working record only after the relevant meeting owner/reviewer confirms them.
- If the meeting output will trigger formal action, preserve the exact human touchpoint needed for confirmation.

## Escalation Triggers
- decision wording is disputed or incomplete
- action owner or deadline cannot be validated
- minutes would imply a formal approval that did not occur
- sensitive/public-impact topic requires tighter review handling
