# Starter Output Examples — Manajemen Risiko (Sauria)

## Example 1 — Risk Register Update
- **Task shape:** identify or update a workflow/project risk.
- **Expected artifact:** risk register note with exposure, impact path, likelihood/severity framing, owner, and mitigation.

## Example 2 — Mitigation Recommendation
- **Task shape:** user asks how to reduce a concrete operational or public-facing risk.
- **Expected artifact:** mitigation note listing available controls, tradeoffs, and decision owner.

## Example 3 — Escalation Memo
- **Task shape:** risk is material and unresolved, with public/legal/operational consequences.
- **Expected artifact:** escalation memo naming the risk, blocked control path, accountable office, and immediate next step.
