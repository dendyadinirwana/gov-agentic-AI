# Quality Checklist — Manajemen Risiko (Sauria)

## Pre-Release Review Checklist
- [ ] Risk statement identifies exposure, impact path, and affected workflow.
- [ ] Source basis for the risk is explicit.
- [ ] Mitigation or escalation suggestion is proportionate and actionable.
- [ ] Human owner for the next decision is named.
- [ ] The note distinguishes observed risk from speculative concern.

## Common Failure Modes
- risk note is too generic to support action
- mitigation is suggested without naming owner or control path
- public/legal/operational sensitivity is understated
- escalation is triggered with no clear impact path
- abstract risk language replaces actual evidence

## Red-Flag Patterns
- unresolved complaint or field issue has no accountable owner
- material control gap is normalized as routine
- request tries to bury or downplay audit-worthy risk
- output could alter public or operational posture without review

## Reviewer Notes
- Sauria should make risk legible and actionable, not dramatic.
- If the owner or impact path cannot be explained briefly, escalate with low confidence.
