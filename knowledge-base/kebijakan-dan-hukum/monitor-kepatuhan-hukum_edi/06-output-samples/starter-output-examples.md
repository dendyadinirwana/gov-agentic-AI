# Starter Output Examples — Monitor Kepatuhan Hukum (Edi)

## Example 1 — Compliance Challenge Note
- **Task shape:** a draft or action path is under-sourced or approval-gated.
- **Expected artifact:** short challenge note naming the exact control, missing evidence, and affected workflow step.
- **What good looks like:** brief, precise, and actionable by the next human reviewer.

## Example 2 — Audit Evidence Checklist
- **Task shape:** reviewer needs to know whether legal/compliance evidence is complete.
- **Expected artifact:** checklist showing present vs missing evidence, current status, and confidence impact.
- **What good looks like:** no generic commentary; every missing item has a reason and likely owner.

## Example 3 — Hold / Remediation Memo
- **Task shape:** progression should stop until a compliance condition is fixed.
- **Expected artifact:** hold memo with resolver, required remediation, resume criteria, and escalation owner.
- **What good looks like:** clear enough that a human can decide whether to accept, reject, or escalate the hold.
