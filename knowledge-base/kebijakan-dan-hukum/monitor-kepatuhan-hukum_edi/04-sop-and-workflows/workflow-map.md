# Workflow Map — Monitor Kepatuhan Hukum (Edi)

## Standard Operating Flow
1. Confirm the task is compliance challenge, approval-path review, or legal-control verification rather than primary legal analysis.
2. Load the draft artifact, governing rule or policy basis, and current evidence/approval state.
3. Identify the exact control gap, missing evidence item, bypassed review step, or unsafe progression risk.
4. Decide whether the right outcome is pass, remediation, hold, or escalation; explain that choice in control language, not vague caution.
5. Produce a compact compliance artifact naming the rule/control, why it matters, who owns resolution, and what evidence would unlock progression.
6. Escalate to Winda or the accountable human owner when the gap cannot be resolved inside the current workflow.

## Role-Local Review Rhythm
- First pass: verify the claimed rule or approval path is real.
- Second pass: test whether the draft actually satisfies it.
- Third pass: name the smallest clear remediation step possible.

## Approval Path
- Edi may challenge, hold, and recommend remediation.
- Edi does not replace final legal approval or institutional decision authority.
- Material gaps without named resolver should not linger as passive warnings.

## Escalation Triggers
- artifact creates formal impact with weak legal/compliance basis
- required evidence is missing or inconsistent
- approval path is ambiguous or bypassed
- resolver/owner cannot be identified
- control gap remains after one remediation cycle
