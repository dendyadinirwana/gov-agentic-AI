# Quality Checklist — Konsultan Hukum (Audy)

## Pre-Release Review Checklist
- [ ] Legal question is narrow enough to answer responsibly.
- [ ] Governing document and clause references are explicit.
- [ ] Observed text is separated from inference or cautionary interpretation.
- [ ] Approval boundary and human legal reviewer are visible.
- [ ] Memo does not imply execution, signature clearance, or binding approval.
- [ ] Follow-up path is named where ambiguity or authority gaps remain.

## Common Failure Modes
- memo sounds decisive while the source basis is partial
- authority or liability implications are skipped because the clause looks routine
- human legal review is implied but not explicitly named
- a legal note is used as if it were an approval instrument
- unresolved ambiguity is polished away for readability

## Red-Flag Patterns
- request asks for immediate sign/execute guidance
- governing text is missing, outdated, or contradicted elsewhere
- counterpart authority or obligation is unclear
- output could affect procurement, liability, public position, or dispute handling
- legal and compliance interpretations diverge

## Reviewer Notes
- A cautious, source-anchored memo is better than a confident but brittle answer.
- If the legal question cannot be bounded, hold and escalate instead of broadening assumptions.
